# Money

This file describes the current stars currency, how it is earned and what it pays for.

## Currency and income

- Stars are the game's only spendable currency; ranking points are a separate progression measure and cannot be spent. ✅
- A new athlete starts with 140 stars, and the balance cannot go below zero during normal play. ✅
- Completing a training mini-game awards stars equal to the rounded score divided by 22, from 0 to 5 stars. ✅
- A race awards a share of its listed maximum prize according to placement, rounded to whole stars; a win adds another 20 stars. ✅
- Race prize shares are 100% for first, 78% for second, 62% for third, 46% for fourth or fifth, 30% for sixth through tenth, 16% through the top 60% of the field and 8% for the rest. ✅
- The advertised race prize excludes the extra 20-star win bonus, and the entry fee is paid before the result is awarded. ✅
- Ending a week pays 18 stars plus one star for each complete 460 places improved from world rank 7000, with no negative bonus. ✅
- The sponsor payout is calculated from the world rank held when the week ends and is paid even when the week is ended early. ✅
- A random weekly sponsor media event awards 40 stars and reduces morale by 4. ✅
- A random weekly local-club event awards 15 stars and increases morale by 4. ✅
- Each weekly event has a 42% chance of occurring overall; when one occurs, one of five events is selected at random. ✅
- One-time ranking-point milestones award 70 stars at 250 points, 120 at 700, 180 at 1,400, 260 at 2,400 and 360 at 3,600. ✅

## Spending

- Travel costs stars based on straight-line distance between the cities' fixed map coordinates: 10 plus 13 times the distance, rounded, with a minimum of 8. ✅
- The destination selector shows each travel price from the current city; travel also consumes one weekly action. ✅
- Entering a race costs its listed entry fee and one weekly action; entry is unavailable without enough stars. ✅
- Swim, bike and run gear upgrades cost 45 plus 55 times the current gear level in stars. ✅
- Upgrading from level 1 through level 6 costs 100, 155, 210, 265 and 320 stars respectively for each gear category. ✅
- Gear upgrades are permanent, increase morale by 1, do not consume an action and stop at level 6. ✅
- If a travel or race attempt fails its affordability check, no stars are spent and the attempted action is returned. ✅
